Network · Appamania

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Network is a game made by Appamania ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: there are no accounts, your progress stays on your phone, and the only data that leaves your phone goes to Google (for ads, crash reports and optional analytics) and to Apple or Google (for purchases).

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, scores, earned themes), your settings and any run in progress are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them; Settings → Reset progress clears them at any time.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. There is no sign-up and no account.

Services that receive data

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google AdMobAdvertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactionsTo show the optional rewarded ad that gives a second chance in the free version. Ads are removed by the Full Network purchase.
Firebase Crashlytics (Google)Crash reports: device model, operating system version, app version, the state of the app when it crashedTo find and fix crashes.
Firebase Analytics (Google)Anonymous gameplay events (for example a level completed, a run ended, a purchase started) and device informationTo learn which levels are too hard or too easy. Off unless you turn on Settings → Share anonymous analytics.
Firebase Remote Config (Google)An app instance identifier and app versionTo download new levels and daily challenges.
Apple App Store / Google PlayPurchase status of the Full Network unlockTo deliver and restore your purchase. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see them.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads, tracking and consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. On iPhone, the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are not personalised and the game works the same. You can change your choice in your device's settings at any time.

Children

Network is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you reset or delete the app. Data held by Google is kept under Google's retention settings: crash reports for up to 90 days, analytics events for up to 14 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn analytics off (it is off by default) in Settings → Share anonymous analytics.
  • Limit ad tracking in your device's privacy settings, or remove ads with the Full Network purchase.
  • Clear your progress with Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we hold no account and no personal data on our own servers, most data can only be linked to you by Google; contact us and we will help with any request.

Security

Data sent to Google, Apple and the app stores travels over encrypted connections. Your progress on your phone is protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Network is a game made by Appamania ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: there are no accounts with us, we run no servers of our own, and the only data that leaves your phone goes to Google (for ads, crash reports, optional analytics and new content) and to Apple or Google (for purchases, and on iPhone for your iCloud backup and Game Center).

Your progress

Your progress (levels, stars, scores, earned themes) and your settings are saved on your device. On iPhone, a copy of your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account, so it comes back if you delete and reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID. That copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud, not by us; we never receive it. Your settings travel with it; a run in progress stays on the device only.

Settings → Reset progress clears your progress on the phone and in iCloud. Deleting the app on iPhone leaves the iCloud copy in place; to remove it, use Reset progress before deleting, or turn off iCloud for the game in your iPhone settings. On Android, progress is kept on the device only.

What we do not collect

No name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. There is no sign-up and no account.

Ads, tracking and consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. On iPhone, the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are not personalised and the game works the same. You can change your choice in your device's settings at any time.

Children

Network is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you reset or delete the app. The iCloud copy stays until you reset progress or remove it from iCloud. Game Center scores and achievements are kept by Apple under its own terms. Data held by Google is kept under Google's retention settings: crash reports for up to 90 days, analytics events for up to 14 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn analytics off (it is off by default) in Settings → Share anonymous analytics.
  • Limit ad tracking in your device's privacy settings, or remove ads with the Full Network purchase.
  • Clear your progress, on the phone and in iCloud, with Settings → Reset progress.
  • Stop sharing scores by signing out of Game Center in your iPhone settings; the game works the same without it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we hold no account and no personal data on our own servers, most data can only be linked to you by Google; contact us and we will help with any request.

Security

Data sent to Google, Apple and the app stores travels over encrypted connections. Your progress on your phone is protected by your device's own security, and the iCloud copy by your Apple ID.

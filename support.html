Support

Help with the game, purchases and your progress.

I bought Full Network. How do I get it back?

Open Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID (or Google account) you bought it with. On Android it usually comes back on its own.

I deleted the game. Is my progress gone?

No. Your purchase is never lost: use Restore. On iPhone, your levels and stars are kept in your iCloud account and come back on their own when you reinstall, signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud on. On Android, progress is saved on the phone; Google's app backup usually brings it back when you reinstall with the same account.

Leaderboards and achievements

On iPhone, sign in to Game Center (iPhone Settings → Game Center) to see how your Endless and daily scores rank, and to earn an achievement for every chapter you finish. Open them from the daily challenge screen. The game works the same without Game Center.

A level feels impossible

Every level is tested to be winnable. Try picking the card for your busiest box, link every door to a worker and every worker to storage, and drag storage closer to the workers when answers are slow. Tap a red box twice to boost it.

Turning off ads or analytics

Analytics is off unless you turn it on in Settings. Ads only appear as an optional reward for a second chance, and Full Network removes them.
